# OpenSGG research terminology

OpenSGG distinguishes evidence-backed baseline reproduction from controlled
synthetic research on relational representations. The reproduction vocabulary is
defined in [reproduction/glossary.md](reproduction/glossary.md).

## Relational emergence

**Observable relational state (S)**: The observable configuration of objects at
one moment, including their relative geometry.

**Interaction mechanism (M)**: A latent response law governing what happens
when objects interact or receive an intervention.
_Avoid_: semantic predicate, relation label when referring to the physical law.

**Mechanism code**: A representation inferred from interaction experience that
may carry information about the latent response law.
_Avoid_: snapshot state code.

**Unilateral contact**: A constraint resisting approach into a material surface.
_Avoid_: support when naming the latent mechanism.

**Bilateral coupling**: A constraint preserving a pair's relative displacement
in both directions along each constrained axis. Bilateral describes the
constraint, not reciprocal forces.
_Avoid_: attachment when naming the latent mechanism.

**Confining boundary**: A constraint restricting an object to a bounded region.
_Avoid_: containment when naming the latent mechanism.

**Free mechanism**: The absence of a pair interaction constraint.

**Base counterfactual context**: Shared fillers, initial state, intervention and
noise allocation under which compatible mechanisms are compared.

**Mechanism exposure**: An opportunity for the response laws to be distinguished
through observed interaction. An absence of response can itself be informative.

**Sealed audit world**: A reserved independent allocation whose results have not
been inspected and whose use requires a frozen protocol.
