# Phase I v2 development boundary and preservation of v1

Status: accepted for development; **not an audit protocol freeze**.

[Issue #107](https://github.com/Aveouter/Open_Sence_Graph_Generation/issues/107)
changes the predictive task, mechanism-code definition and controls. Preserve
[PR #106](https://github.com/Aveouter/Open_Sence_Graph_Generation/pull/106) at
`148b25663a40f8f4b2385956166859efebec8a81` as the immutable v1 diagnostic record.
Its Phase IB reports are superseded for confirmatory claims. In particular, the
v1 null transplant cannot establish that a decoder ignored mechanism information:
its snapshot source code could not contain that information in the first place.
Existing v1 code, reports, ADRs and local artifacts are left intact.

Use `relational_emergence.phase1.v2.dev1` for the new development candidate.
Reserve generator seed `2027021702` and families `v2_audit_0` through
`v2_audit_3` for a future audit; development uses seed `2026092302` and families
`v2_dev_0` through `v2_dev_3`. Allocation does not authorize opening the audit.
No v2 audit data is generated by the development entry point. An eventual freeze
must bind the successful world, trained-decoder controls, Level-2, complete seed
factorial, thresholds and code/data/config digests. Inspected v1 audit data can
never supply these requirements.

The four-body candidate reuses the audited pair laws with three probes sharing
one externally driven supporter. This preserves the existing kinematic
assumption and gives the model multiple directed influences, but it is a star
graph: probes do not collide and there is no backreaction on the supporter.
Two independently drawn contexts per filler permit within-filler transplants.
Object slots vary by base context. Mechanistic names are mapped at the v1
physics boundary; historical semantic names are not retroactively relabeled.

The representation contract separates observable snapshot state from history
inference. History stops at the inference cutoff; labels, activation metadata
and future targets remain outside the model inputs. All arms share the current
prediction state and future actions. Initial-static and post-state codes receive
only their named position snapshot and physical attributes. Thus these are
controls on the **code's experience**, not claims that the entire predictor has
never observed a post-interaction state.

Random permutations preserve frame/action content and remove the fixed reversal
shortcut. They do not guarantee that physics has erased chronology. The separate
held-out chronology classifier can therefore veto temporal-teaching claims even
when the permutation code is correct. Its null is not promoted to a PASS without
power calibration.

M0 uses trained MLP and linear probes on six feature sets, training-only scaling,
base-group splits, compatible group-level label permutations and a family maximum
test. Permutations condition on models trained without test labels; they do not
refit models. CE and cluster bootstrap statistics use double precision and a
`1e-12` numerical tie tolerance. This repairs a development false positive from
float32 accumulation, exposed by a balanced-twin regression test; it does not
change the practical MDE of `0.05` nats. Graded nonlinear erasure controls have
known Bayes information of `0.025`, `0.05` and `0.20` nats; randomized-label
controls must stay null. Twenty replicates estimate power, with an empirical
target of 0.8. This is a calibration estimate, not a confidence-bound guarantee.

For this candidate, the world check conservatively requires every rich-regime
compatible cell to exceed its measured self-divergence floor, and the prediction
and history windows must cover the slowest measured 90%-peak horizon. These
development checks are stricter than a claim that some cells are identifiable;
they do not inherit a v1 GO. No failed context is silently removed from a
representation result. Zero-sensitivity transplant pairs stay in the primary
statistic; disjoint source-target context dyads are bootstrapped together.

The implementation and measured blockers are recorded in
[the v2 development report](../../outputs/reports/relational_emergence/v2/01_development_gate.md).
Failed prerequisites leave #107 open. Level-2, real learned-decoder calibration,
the multi-seed representation factorial and a fresh audit remain subsequent
work, rather than a substitute result from a smoke run.
